# Omnis — Main Purpose

**This document defines what Omnis IS. Every design, copy, and product
decision in the redesign must serve this. The docs/knowledge base is the
supporting cast — this is the lead.**

---

## The one-sentence thesis

Omnis is a modern, cross-platform interface for reading yourself and every
person in your life through five wisdom systems at once — and a **living,
persistent map** of the connections and group dynamics between all of them.

## The five pillars

### 1. One person, five systems, one screen
Enter a birth (date, time, place, name) and get the full picture in one
place: Astrology natal chart, Dreamspell galactic signature, Tzolkin day
sign, Human Design bodygraph, Hebrew Gematria. No tab-hopping between five
different single-system sites. Layered or side by side.

### 2. The map — relationships made visible
Every person you chart becomes a node. Omnis draws the connections:
compatibility scores, synastry, kin resonance, channel electromagnetics —
across all five systems. You *see* the geometry of your people: who
harmonizes, who challenges, who completes.

### 3. Group dynamics — layered or fused
Select any group of people — family, team, friend circle, band — and read
its dynamic through one system at a time (layers) or all five fused into a
single group reading. Notice the *different nature* of each circle in your
life: why the family feels one way and the startup another.

### 4. Persistence — enter a birthday once, forever
The quiet superpower. No more re-asking a friend's birth time for the 10th
time before making them a chart. Everyone you've ever charted is saved,
owned by you, instantly reusable in any new map, group, or reading. Your
people-library compounds in value with every entry.

### 5. Collaboration & gifting
Maps aren't solitary. Invite collaborators to view and explore your maps.
Build a map *for* someone — their family, their team — and send it to them
as a shareable artifact. Readings become gifts; the share link is the
growth loop.

## What the knowledge base is for

The `/learn` docs (each flavored with its system's folklore) exist to make
the readings *trustworthy and deep* — the curated source that explains what
a Gate 34 or a Kin 113 or a chesed-heavy name actually means. Docs feed the
product; the product is the map.

## Positioning line

> Single-system sites give you a reading. Omnis gives you your world —
> everyone you love, mapped across five traditions, remembered forever.

## Hierarchy for all surfaces (homepage, app, share pages)

1. The map / dynamics (hero value)
2. Five-system personal reading (entry point)
3. Persistence (retention promise)
4. Collaboration/sharing (growth loop)
5. Knowledge docs (trust & depth)
