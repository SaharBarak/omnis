# Omnis Homepage — Copy, Composition & Structure (v2)

**Produced by multi-provider synthesis (Codex + OpenCode + Claude drafts →
cross-critique → resolution). Replaces the rejected v1.**

The spine is the user's life, widening outward: **you → you + one → your
people → your circles → beyond you.** The hero promises the map; the map
itself lands mid-page as the single biggest moment. The five systems are
lenses, not products; docs are the trust layer.

One continuous mural (cosmos descending to parchment); thread of light +
traveler glyph ride the full descent; flavor zones follow the mural, and
the MAP zone is where all five flavor threads visibly converge.

## Copy voice rules
- Serif headlines: complete thoughts a visitor *feels*, never feature
  labels. Calm, concrete, confident.
- No mysticism kitsch ("unlock your destiny"), no SaaS hype
  ("supercharge"), no fantasy-speak ("five worlds").
- Dry humor allowed exactly once — the persistence pain line.
- Every zone exits into the knowledge base (trust loop).

## Why v1 failed (consensus of three models)
1. Map buried at zone 3 — page read as a calculator, wrong mental model
   formed by the third scroll.
2. Zone names were internal feature labels, not propositions.
3. Copy informational, not evocative — README bullets vs "Ship software
   peacefully".
4. Persistence under-voiced; the relatable pain never named.
5. Folklore-per-feature pairings rationalized (tzolkin=records) instead
   of felt; flavors now follow the mural and converge at the map.
6. No single-line thesis; hero deferred the promise.
