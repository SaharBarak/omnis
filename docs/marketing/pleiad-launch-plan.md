## The product, in one line

**Map the people who shape your life** — a living, persistent map of everyone
who matters to you, read across multiple wisdom systems (Astrology, Human
Design, Dreamspell/Tzolkin, Gematria/Kabbalah…), with fused group dynamics,
gifting, and collaboration. Single readings are the entry point; the knowledge
docs are the trust layer; **the map is the product.**

## Positioning (steer this)

- **Category:** not "another astrology app." Pleiad is *relationship
  intelligence* through ancient systems — a map of your people, not a horoscope.
- **Wedge:** the fused **group/compatibility** reading (nobody else maps *layered
  group dynamics* across systems). Lead with it — it's the "whoa."
- **Trust:** the `/learn` docs (Dreamspell, Tzolkin, Human Design, Astrology,
  Gematria, integration) are the credibility layer that separates Pleiad from
  entertainment apps — and they're AEO/SEO assets (already crawlable, sitemap
  submitted).

## The offer — Founding Lifetime ($79 once)

Already live. This is your launch lever (per the "lifetime deal" reel):
- Early capital to reinvest + fast feedback from committed users.
- Scarcity/exclusivity framing: "Founding" members, capped or time-boxed.
- Channels: your own list first; **AppSumo** later if you lean practitioner/B2B.

## ORB channels

**Owned (start here — you already have two):**
- **Email** — the *Daily Galactic Signature* newsletter already ships. This is
  the flywheel: free daily value → warm list → launch + Founding Lifetime offer.
- **`/learn` knowledge docs** — SEO/AEO trust content; keep expanding, they
  compound and get cited by AI answers (robots.txt already allows AI crawlers).
- **Add:** a small community (Discord) for Founding members — feedback + word of mouth.

**Rented (pick 1–2 — your audience skews visual/spiritual):**
- **Instagram / TikTok** — short vertical video is where this audience lives (it's
  literally where these launch reels came from). Post map walkthroughs, "read my
  group" clips, system explainers → drive to the email list.
- **X** — build-in-public + astrology/HD threads → newsletter.

**Borrowed:**
- Astrology / Human Design **podcasters & creators** — partner readings, guest
  spots, affiliate Founding Lifetime codes.
- **Practitioner partnerships** — astrologers/HD readers who'd use team
  workspaces + external readings (ties to EPIC-002/003); they bring their audiences.

## Five-phase rollout (you're at Phase 3–4)

1. **Internal** ✓ — product live at pleiad.io on the (dev-as-prod) stack.
2. **Alpha** ✓ — landing + calculator are public; newsletter capturing signups.
3. **Beta (now):** open Founding Lifetime to the email list + a small invite ring;
   tease the *group reading* with GIFs/clips; recruit 10–20 practitioners.
4. **Early Access:** leak map/group screenshots + demos; run a short PMF survey;
   incentivize feedback with credits; batch-invite or open under "early access."
5. **Full launch:** self-serve signups + **Paddle LIVE** (the gate), GA
   announcement across all channels, Product Hunt.

## Product Hunt playbook (Phase 5)

- **Prep 2–4 weeks out:** line up a credible hunter; build assets (gallery GIF of
  a group reading, 3–5 screenshots, a 30–60s demo, first comment/maker story).
- **Offer:** exclusive PH deal (extend/discount Founding Lifetime for launch day).
- **Launch day:** 12:01 PT post; rally the email list + community first (owned
  drives the spike); maker actively answers every comment; cross-post to X/IG.
- **Don't rely on it** — PH is a spike, the email flywheel is the engine.

## Launch checklist (pre-GA gates)

- [ ] Paddle **LIVE** (real charges) — blocks charging on full launch
- [ ] Turnstile secret set (bot-protect the signup/newsletter surface)
- [ ] Email deliverability: Resend domain verified + SPF/DKIM/DMARC on pleiad.io
- [ ] Founding Lifetime scarcity framing finalized (cap or deadline)
- [ ] Group-reading demo asset (GIF + 60s video)
- [ ] `/learn` docs expanded for the top 5 search/AEO queries
- [ ] North Star live in PostHog (Weekly Active Mappers) — watch activation, not signups
- [ ] PH hunter + assets ready
- [ ] Community (Discord) stood up for Founding members

## North Star tie-in

Optimize the whole launch for **Weekly Active Mappers** (people who add a person
or run a group reading weekly) — not raw signups. A Founding Lifetime buyer who
never maps anyone isn't value. See `docs/analytics/north-star.md`.
