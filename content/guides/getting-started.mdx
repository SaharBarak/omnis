# Getting Started with Omnis

Welcome to Omnis! This guide will walk you through setting up your account and exploring your cosmic profile for the first time.

## Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the Omnis website** and click "Get Started" or "Sign Up"
2. **Enter your email** and create a secure password
3. **Verify your email** by clicking the link sent to your inbox
4. **Complete your profile** — you'll be prompted to add your birth data

> 💡 **Tip:** Use an email you check regularly. Omnis sends daily insights and transit alerts!

## Adding Your First Person (Birth Data)

Your birth data is the foundation of everything in Omnis. Here's what you'll need:

### Required Information

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Full Name** | Your name (or nickname) | "Sarah" |
| **Birth Date** | Day, month, year | February 15, 1990 |
| **Birth Time** | As precise as possible | 3:42 PM |
| **Birth Location** | City and country | Tel Aviv, Israel |

### How to Add Your Data

1. Navigate to **People** in the sidebar
2. Click **"+ Add Person"**
3. Fill in the birth details
4. Click **"Generate Chart"**

![Screenshot placeholder: Add Person form](/images/guides/add-person-form.png)

> ⚠️ **Birth Time Matters:** The more accurate your birth time, the more precise your Human Design chart and astrological houses will be. Check your birth certificate if possible!

### Don't Know Your Birth Time?

No worries! You can still use Omnis:
- Astrology sun sign and major planets will be accurate
- Human Design Type is often (but not always) correct
- Some features like precise Rising sign won't be available

You can always update your birth time later when you find it.

## Understanding Your Dashboard

Once you've added your birth data, you'll land on your personal dashboard.

### Dashboard Sections

**🌟 Your Profile Summary**
- Your Human Design Type (Generator, Projector, Manifestor, Manifesting Generator, or Reflector)
- Your Strategy and Authority
- Key astrological placements (Sun, Moon, Rising)

**📊 Today's Energies**
- Current transits affecting you
- Gate activations for the day
- Recommended actions based on your design

**🔮 Quick Actions**
- Check compatibility with someone
- View your full chart
- Access today's guidance

## Key Features Overview

### 🧬 Human Design Chart
View your complete bodygraph with all 64 gates, 9 centers, channels, and profile information. Tap any element to learn what it means for you.

### ⭐ Astrological Profile
Your natal chart with houses, aspects, and planetary positions. Includes interpretations tailored to your unique combination.

### 💕 Compatibility Analysis
Compare your chart with partners, friends, family, or colleagues. See where you connect and where friction might arise.

### 📅 Transit Calendar
Track how current planetary movements and gate transits affect your personal chart day by day.

### 📈 Daily Guidance
Personalized recommendations based on what's happening in the cosmos and how it interacts with your design.
