# Compatibility Guide

Discover how you connect with the people in your life. Pleiad analyzes both Human Design and astrological compatibility to give you deep insights into any relationship.

## Adding Multiple People

Before you can run compatibility, you need at least two people in your system.

### Adding People to Your Account

1. Go to **People** in the sidebar
2. Click **"+ Add Person"**
3. Enter their birth details:
   - Name
   - Birth date
   - Birth time (if known)
   - Birth location
4. Click **"Generate Chart"**

![Screenshot placeholder: People list with multiple profiles](/images/guides/people-list.png)

> 💡 **Tip:** You can add as many people as you like — partners, family, friends, colleagues, even celebrities!

### Organizing Your People

- **Star favorites** for quick access
- **Add tags** like "Family," "Work," or "Friends"
- **Add notes** to remember context (e.g., "Met at yoga class")

## Running Compatibility Analysis

### Quick Compatibility Check

1. From any person's profile, click **"Check Compatibility"**
2. Select the second person from your list
3. View your compatibility report instantly

### From the Compatibility Section

1. Navigate to **Compatibility** in the sidebar
2. Select **Person A** from the dropdown
3. Select **Person B** from the dropdown
4. Click **"Analyze"**

![Screenshot placeholder: Compatibility selection screen](/images/guides/compatibility-select.png)

## Understanding Compatibility Scores

Pleiad breaks down compatibility into multiple dimensions:

### Overall Compatibility Score

A percentage showing general alignment. This considers:
- Human Design electromagnetic connections
- Astrological synastry aspects
- Composite chart harmony

> 🎯 **What the scores mean:**
> - **80-100%** — Strong natural resonance
> - **60-79%** — Good compatibility with some growth areas
> - **40-59%** — Balanced mix of harmony and friction
> - **Below 40%** — Challenging dynamics (not bad, just requires awareness!)

### Human Design Compatibility

**Electromagnetic Connections**
When your defined gates connect with their defined gates to complete a channel. These create magnetic attraction!

**Compromise Channels**
Where you both define the same channel differently. Can create friction or depth.

**Dominance Dynamics**
Who has more defined centers? This affects the energy flow in the relationship.

![Screenshot placeholder: HD compatibility bodygraph overlay](/images/guides/hd-compatibility.png)

### Astrological Synastry

**Harmonious Aspects (Green)**
- Trines (120°) — Easy flow
- Sextiles (60°) — Opportunities
- Conjunctions — Intensity (can be positive)

**Challenging Aspects (Red)**
- Squares (90°) — Tension and growth
- Oppositions (180°) — Push-pull dynamics

**Key Connections to Watch**
- Sun-Moon contacts (emotional understanding)
- Venus-Mars contacts (attraction and passion)
- Mercury contacts (communication style)

## Tips for Relationship Insights

### For Romantic Partners

1. **Check Venus-Mars aspects** — These show romantic and physical chemistry
2. **Look at Moon compatibility** — This reveals emotional needs alignment
3. **Review HD Authority interaction** — Do your decision-making styles complement each other?

### For Family Members

1. **Focus on communication** — Mercury aspects show how you'll understand each other
2. **Check for power dynamics** — Defined vs. undefined centers
3. **Look at generational patterns** — Outer planet connections

### For Work Relationships

1. **Mercury and Saturn aspects** — Communication and responsibility
2. **Check for complementary HD Types** — Generators and Projectors often work well together
3. **Look at Mars aspects** — How you'll handle conflict and drive

### For Friendships

1. **Sun-Sun aspects** — Core identity compatibility
2. **Jupiter connections** — Growth and fun together
3. **HD Profile compatibility** — 1st lines often connect with 4th lines, etc.

## Making the Most of Your Insights

### What High Compatibility Means
- Natural understanding
- Less explanation needed
- Easier flow in the relationship

### What Low Compatibility Means
- More growth opportunities
- Requires conscious communication
- Not "bad" — just different!

> 💫 **Remember:** Low compatibility scores don't mean doom! Many successful relationships have "challenging" charts. Awareness is the key. When you know where friction might arise, you can navigate it consciously.

### Action Steps After Viewing Compatibility

1. **Discuss with your person** — Share what resonates
2. **Identify growth areas** — Where can you be more patient?
3. **Celebrate strengths** — What comes naturally between you?
4. **Revisit periodically** — Your understanding deepens over time

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I check compatibility with someone not in my account?**
A: You'll need to add them first. Even partial data (no birth time) gives useful insights.

**Q: Why is my compatibility different from other apps?**
A: Pleiad uses both Human Design AND astrology. Other apps typically use only one system.

**Q: Does compatibility change over time?**
A: The natal compatibility stays the same, but transits can activate different themes at different times. Check back during significant transits!
