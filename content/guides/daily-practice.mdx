# Daily Practice with Pleiad

Make cosmic wisdom part of your everyday life. This guide shows you how to use Pleiad for daily guidance, intention setting, and living in alignment with your design.

## Checking Daily Energies

Every day brings different energetic influences. Here's how to stay informed:

### Morning Check-In (2-3 minutes)

1. **Open Pleiad** first thing (or with your morning coffee ☕)
2. **Go to Today's Energies** on your dashboard
3. **Review the highlights:**
   - What gates are transiting today?
   - Are any activating your chart?
   - What's the overall theme?

![Screenshot placeholder: Today's energies panel](/images/guides/daily-energies.png)

### What You'll See

**🌅 Daily Gate Transit**
The Sun moves through one gate approximately every 5-6 days. This colors the collective energy everyone feels.

**🌙 Emotional Wave**
The Moon transits a gate roughly every 10 hours, creating shorter emotional themes throughout the day.

**⚡ Personal Activations**
When a transiting planet touches a gate in YOUR chart, you feel it more personally. Pleiad highlights these for you.

### Quick Interpretation Guide

| If you see... | It means... | Action |
|---------------|-------------|--------|
| 🟢 Green highlight | Transit supports your design | Lean in, take action |
| 🟡 Yellow highlight | Neutral transit | Observe, stay aware |
| 🔴 Red highlight | Challenging transit | Practice patience, self-care |

## Understanding Transits

Transits are the current positions of planets as they interact with your birth chart. They're like weather — temporary but influential.

### Fast-Moving Transits (Daily-Weekly)

**Sun Transits**
- Duration: ~5-6 days per gate
- Effect: What's illuminated, focus areas
- How to use: Align major activities with supportive sun gates

**Moon Transits**
- Duration: ~10 hours per gate
- Effect: Emotional undertones, intuition
- How to use: Note your emotional patterns through the day

**Mercury Transits**
- Duration: Varies (can retrograde!)
- Effect: Communication, thinking, decisions
- How to use: Plan important conversations during harmonious Mercury

### Slow-Moving Transits (Weeks-Months)

**Mars, Venus, Jupiter, Saturn**
- These create longer themes in your life
- Check weekly rather than daily
- More significant for big decisions and planning

## Setting Intentions with the Calendar

Pleiad's calendar feature helps you plan with cosmic timing on your side.

### How to Use the Transit Calendar

1. Navigate to **Calendar** in the sidebar
2. Select the date range you want to view
3. See transits overlaid on each day
4. **Tap any day** for detailed breakdown

### Planning Tips by Transit

**Best times for...**

| Activity | Look for | Avoid |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Starting projects | New Moon, Sun in Gate 51 (shock/initiation) | Mercury retrograde, void of course Moon |
| Important meetings | Harmonious Mercury transits | Challenging Mars aspects |
| Creative work | Venus transits, Gate 1 (creativity) | Heavy Saturn transits |
| Rest and reflection | Full Moon, Gate 29 (surrender) | Action-oriented transits |

### Creating Intention Entries

1. **Find a supportive transit** for your goal
2. **Tap the day** on the calendar
3. **Click "Add Intention"**
4. **Write your intention** clearly and positively
5. **Set a reminder** if you want

> 💫 **Example Intention:** "I initiate my new project with confidence and clarity" — set during a New Moon in a gate that supports your design.

## Integrating with Your Routine

### The 5-Minute Morning Ritual

1. **Open Pleiad** (1 min)
2. **Read today's energy theme** (1 min)
3. **Check for personal activations** (1 min)
4. **Set one small intention** (1 min)
5. **Close and begin your day** (1 min)

### The Evening Review (Optional)

1. **Revisit your morning intention** — Did it play out?
2. **Note any significant moments** — Correlate with transits
3. **Preview tomorrow** — Anything to prepare for?

### Weekly Practice

Every Sunday (or your preferred day):

1. **Review the week's transits ahead**
2. **Mark important days** in your calendar
3. **Set 1-3 weekly intentions**
4. **Note any challenging transits** — prepare mentally

## Living Your Design Daily

### By Human Design Type

**Generators & Manifesting Generators**
- Morning: Check what lights you up today
- Throughout day: Follow your sacral response
- Evening: Review what gave you satisfaction

**Projectors**
- Morning: Wait for today's invitations
- Throughout day: Share wisdom when recognized
- Evening: Rest deeply, process the day

**Manifestors**
- Morning: Set your intention, inform others
- Throughout day: Initiate what feels aligned
- Evening: Release and let go

**Reflectors**
- Morning: Notice the day's energy theme
- Throughout day: Sample the environment, don't identify
- Evening: Process what surprised you

### By Strategy and Authority

Use your daily check-in to align decisions with your authority:

- **Emotional Authority**: Check moon transits for your emotional wave
- **Sacral Authority**: Tune into gut responses to today's themes
- **Splenic Authority**: Trust in-the-moment instincts
- **Self-Projected**: Talk through decisions with someone
- **Ego Authority**: Check in with what you truly want
- **Mental/Environmental**: Notice what the environment reflects

## Tracking Your Journey

### Daily Notes Feature

1. Go to **Journal** or **Notes**
2. Click **"+ New Entry"**
3. The current transits auto-attach to your entry
4. Write your observations, feelings, insights
5. Over time, see patterns emerge

### What to Track

- Energy levels (1-10)
- Mood throughout the day
- Significant events or decisions
- Dreams (especially during Neptune transits)
- Synchronicities and insights

> 📊 **Pro Tip:** After a month of tracking, review your notes. You'll start to see which transits affect you most strongly!
