What if you've been living your entire life using the wrong strategy?

## 🔥 Manifestors: The Initiators

**Population:** ~9%
**Strategy:** Inform before acting
**Signature:** Peace
**Not-Self Theme:** Anger

Manifestors are the **only type designed to initiate**. They have a closed and repelling aura that allows them to move through the world independently.

### How to Spot a Manifestor
- They make things happen — often before anyone else sees it coming
- They don't wait for permission
- They can feel restricted by having to explain themselves
- When healthy: powerful, impactful, peaceful
- When not aligned: angry, frustrated, misunderstood

### The Manifestor's Gift
Manifestors are here to **start new things**. They're the spark that ignites movements, projects, and change. Their job isn't to do all the work — it's to initiate and then let others carry the energy forward.

### The Manifestor's Challenge
Learning to **inform** (not ask permission!) before acting. This simple shift reduces resistance and allows their impact to flow.

---

## ⚡ Generators: The Life Force

**Population:** ~37%
**Strategy:** Wait to respond
**Signature:** Satisfaction
**Not-Self Theme:** Frustration

Generators are the **builders of the world**. They have an open, enveloping aura that pulls life toward them. They have consistent access to sacral (life force) energy.

### How to Spot a Generator
- They have sustainable energy when doing what they love
- They need to be lit up by something before they can commit
- They often say "uh-huh" or "nuh-uh" — their sacral response
- When healthy: satisfied, energized, lit up
- When not aligned: frustrated, stuck, drained

### The Generator's Gift
When a Generator is doing work that truly lights them up, they have **endless energy**. They're designed to master skills and become experts through repetitive engagement.

### The Generator's Challenge
Learning to **wait for something to respond to** instead of initiating. The sacral response (that gut feeling) is their compass — but it only works when responding to life, not forcing direction.

---

## ⚡🔥 Manifesting Generators: The Multi-Passionate

**Population:** ~33%
**Strategy:** Wait to respond, then inform
**Signature:** Satisfaction
**Not-Self Theme:** Frustration & Anger

Manifesting Generators (MG's) are a hybrid — they have the initiating capacity of Manifestors and the sustainable sacral energy of Generators.

### How to Spot a Manifesting Generator
- They do everything fast — and skip steps
- They often have multiple passions and projects
- They pivot quickly when something isn't right
- When healthy: satisfied, efficient, multi-passionate
- When not aligned: scattered, frustrated, impatient

### The MG's Gift
Manifesting Generators are designed to **do multiple things** (despite what society tells them). They find efficiency through non-linear processes and often become the fastest at mastering skills.

### The MG's Challenge
Still needing to **wait to respond** before acting. When MGs initiate from the mind instead of waiting for that sacral hit, they end up in wrong commitments.

---

## 👁️ Projectors: The Guides

**Population:** ~20%
**Strategy:** Wait for the invitation
**Signature:** Success
**Not-Self Theme:** Bitterness

Projectors are here to **guide and direct the energy of others**. They have a focused, penetrating aura that can see deeply into people and systems.

### How to Spot a Projector
- They see things others miss
- They're often the "wise friend" everyone goes to for advice
- They don't have sustainable energy for grunt work
- When healthy: successful, recognized, fulfilled
- When not aligned: bitter, exhausted, invisible

### The Projector's Gift
Projectors are natural **leaders and guides** — not through doing, but through directing. When they're invited into the right roles, their insights can transform entire organizations.

### The Projector's Challenge
**Waiting for recognition and invitation** before sharing their wisdom. Unsolicited advice from a Projector usually lands flat — but when invited? Magic happens.

---

## 🌙 Reflectors: The Mirrors

**Population:** ~1%
**Strategy:** Wait a lunar cycle (28 days)
**Signature:** Surprise
**Not-Self Theme:** Disappointment

Reflectors are the rarest type. They have **no defined centers** in their chart, meaning they take in and amplify the energy around them.

### How to Spot a Reflector
- They're deeply affected by their environment
- They seem different depending on who they're with
- They have unique wisdom about the health of communities
- When healthy: surprised by life, wise, connected to the moon
- When not aligned: disappointed, lost, invisible

### The Reflector's Gift
Reflectors are here to **reflect the health of their community**. When a Reflector is thriving, it's a sign that the environment is healthy. They have access to profound wisdom about what's working and what isn't.

### The Reflector's Challenge
Taking **a full lunar cycle (28 days)** before making major decisions. This allows them to experience something from all perspectives as the moon moves through the gates.
